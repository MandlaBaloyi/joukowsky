VALIDATION STRATEGY
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 - for review

Where the tests live (added 2026-09-28)
---------------------------------------
tests/<context>/<rung>/<topic>/ in the repository (crate jk-tests; see
tests/README.md). Rungs map to folders: 1 analytical/, invariants/ (laws
that must hold in every run; property tests), 3 cross_code/, 4 datasets/,
5 golden/. A test proving a case below starts with its ID (va1_, va2_,
...). Slow or exhaustive tests are marked #[ignore] and run in the full
suite: .\tests\run-tests.ps1 -Suite full.

Ladder (every algorithm climbs every rung)
------------------------------------------
Rung 1  Analytical: results that must match closed-form physics.
  V-A1  Instantaneous valve closure, reservoir-pipe-valve: peak = Joukowsky,
        period = 4L/a, square wave (frictionless).
  V-A2  Slow closure: compare with Michaud estimate and published
        benchmark solutions.
  V-A3  Junction transmission/reflection coefficients.
  V-A4  Mass conservation to machine precision in network MOC.
  V-A5  Viscoelastic (plastic) wall: ring frequency and decay rate of a
        reservoir-pipe-valve system vs the exact complex-frequency
        solution; first-order convergence, extrapolated within 0.5%
        (2026-09-29: +0.1% at 400 reaches after FN-021).
  V-A12 Rule-based controls: two rules that copy the V-A9 level controls
        switch the pump at the same moments (difference 0 s); a priority-5
        clock window holds the pump off while a priority-1 level rule wants
        it on, and the level rule wins the moment the window ends; OR binds
        tighter than AND (EPANET 2.2 manual); the V-A9 file with [RULES]
        instead of [CONTROLS] runs identically (2026-09-30).
  V-A11 Other control valves: a PSV holding a high zone at 70 m while it
        feeds a low reservoir (bisection of the energy balance), fully open
        when the reservoir is above its setting; an FCV passing exactly its
        setting, fully open above the natural flow; a TCV as sqrt(dH /
        sum R) both ways (2026-09-30: all to 1e-8 or better).
  V-A10 Pressure-reducing valves: ACTIVE / OPEN / CLOSED heads in closed
        form, and a closed PRV reopening to share when a second source
        cannot hold the zone; a wave crossing the frozen valve vs the
        orifice law (2026-09-30: all to 1e-9 or better; 71% of a 0.5 m wave
        passes a valve dropping 30 m at 50 l/s). Also read from EPANET
        files (SI and US units: elevation + setting, psi at 0.4333 per ft)
        and a 5 s transient from its steady state stays within 4e-11 m.
  V-A9  Controls (extended period): a pump filling a tank between switch
        levels, a float valve on a tank inlet - switch times vs Simpson
        quadrature of A dh / (Q_in(h) - D) and the straight drain with the
        inlet shut; load-shedding windows; daily clock switches; a closed
        pipe = the pipe removed (2026-09-30: switches within 0.05 s over
        48 h with 5-min steps, 2.4 s with 15-min steps; closed pipe 1e-12 m).
        The same system written as an EPANET file (pump curve, controls,
        times) and read by jk-files: switches within 5e-6 of the reference
        (tests/adapters/analytical/epanet_operations.rs).
  V-A8  Storage tanks (extended period): draining tank vs the exact
        sqrt-law; empty time vs volume / demand (2026-09-30: 0.12 mm over
        12 h, second order; empty time exact).
  V-A7  Source level changing with time (reservoir history): rigid-column
        theory Q = g A r t^2 / (2L) for a slowly rising source (2026-09-30:
        exact at multiples of 2L/a, 0.05% between).
  V-A6  Viscoelastic wall, sharp front: jump height decays as exp(-kappa x)
        with kappa = sum a c J / (g tau); fitted kappa within 5%
        (2026-09-29: 1.4%).
Rung 2  Published benchmarks and experiments.
  V-B1  Classic laboratory data (e.g. Adelaide / Bergant-Simpson
        reservoir-pipe-valve experiments) for friction and column
        separation models [identify exact datasets].
  V-B2  BattLeDIM (L-Town) for network-scale leak detection at SCADA
        rates - useful for slow-leak methods and the digital twin, NOT for
        transient methods (its data is not high-frequency).
  V-B3  Search for open high-frequency network transient datasets
        (research backlog R-3). If none exist, ours becomes a contribution.
Rung 3  Simulation studies.
  V-S1  Sampling-rate and clock-error study: localisation error vs f_s and
        sigma_clock on synthetic networks. Confirms 03_ targets.
  V-S2  Detection coverage vs sensor count per network archetype.
        STATUS 2026-09-28: first build - junction-by-junction front model,
        within 0.05% of the MOC simulation on the estate; upper bound until
        plastic-wall damping (S6). friction_and_damping.txt, note at end.
  V-S3  Classifier trained on synthetic data, tested on held-out
        networks (generalisation).
  V-E1..V-E6  External factors (lightning spike on one logger, restart
        surge, wrong wave speed from temperature, vibration noise, earth
        tremor on all loggers at once): 10_findings/external_factors.txt.
  V-S4  Scale: synthetic towns of 10 / 50 / 100 km - time the simulator,
        live engine and advisor (10_findings/detection_range_and_scale.txt,
        gap G-10).
Rung 4  Public and partner datasets (software only - we build no rig).
  Published lab and field transient datasets (08_research/open_questions
  R-3), plus recorded data shared by sensor vendors, utilities or
  universities. Lab data gives ground truth; field data gives realism.
Rung 5  Live partner data.
  Run the engine in listen-only (shadow) mode on a live data feed from a
  partner's sensors. Ask the partner to log known events (pump starts,
  valve operations, opening a hydrant at a known place and time) to
  measure real localisation error and false-alarm rate.

Metrics
-------
  Detection:       probability of detection vs event amplitude (curve);
                   detection delay distribution.
  False alarms:    per sensor-day, after calibration period.
  Classification:  per-class precision/recall, calibration error,
                   abstention rate.
  Localisation:    distance error [m] along pipe; top-k segment accuracy;
                   credible-region coverage (does the 90% region contain
                   the truth ~90% of the time?).
  Quantification:  burst flow estimate vs metered truth.
  Safety:          simulated max pressure of every recommended action.
  Engineering:     processing latency, throughput per stream, behaviour
                   with gaps / late / out-of-order data.
