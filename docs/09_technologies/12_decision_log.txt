TD-017 | 2026-09-28 | Pumps, version 1: normal operating zone only
       (affinity laws on the 3-point datasheet curve, parabolic efficiency),
       with an integral discharge non-return valve. Pumps without one, or
       reverse flow / turbining / reverse rotation, need four-quadrant
       (Suter) curves: version 2. The run-down is Newton's law for
       rotation; inertia defaults to Thorley's regressions and is treated
       as an uncertain prior (-50%/+100%), to be learned from real trips.
       Leaving the normal zone during a simulated trip is detected and
       reported, not hidden. | Claude (delegated) | covers the standard SA
       pump-station layout; TSNet has no inertia-driven power-failure
       model at all, so this is a capability, and complete-characteristic
       data is rarely available for real pumps. |
