STATISTICS AND INFERENCE
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 - for review

Principle P3 makes uncertainty a first-class output. That puts Bayesian
inference at the centre of localisation, the digital twin and inverse
transient analysis - not as an add-on.

1. Samplers and inference engines
------------------------------------------------------------------------
nuts-rs (PyMC developers)                                        [TRIAL]
  ROLE  No-U-Turn Hamiltonian Monte Carlo for posteriors over network
        parameters (wave speeds, friction, leak sizes) in ITA and twin
        calibration.
  WHY   Rust-native, mature enough to be the engine behind nutpie
        (PyMC's fast sampler). Needs the gradient of the log-density -
        which our discrete adjoint (02_ S8) supplies.
  EXIT  Our own HMC; the algorithm is well documented.

Sequential Monte Carlo (particle filters, SMC samplers)          [BUILD]
  ROLE  Event-by-event updating of per-pipe parameters (05_learning_
        systems/digital_twin.txt); tempering for multi-modal ITA
        posteriors where HMC gets stuck between modes.

Ensemble Kalman filter                                           [BUILD]
  ROLE  Slow hydraulic state assimilation from 1 Hz summaries.

Grid posterior over the network                                  [BUILD]
  ROLE  The localisation posterior p(x0 | arrival times) evaluated on a
        fine grid along every edge, with t0 eliminated analytically and
        wave-speed uncertainty marginalised by Monte Carlo. Exact, fast,
        and explainable - the primary localiser before any ML.

2. Calibration of uncertainty
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Conformal prediction                                             [BUILD]
  ROLE  Distribution-free guarantees on classifier sets and location
        regions ("the 90% region really contains the truth 90% of the
        time"). Small algorithm; we implement it and test coverage.
Temperature scaling / isotonic calibration                       [BUILD]
Coverage testing                                                 [ADOPT
                                                             as practice]
  Every credible region the engine emits is checked for empirical
  coverage on simulation and labelled events (07_ metrics).

3. Damage and risk statistics
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rainflow counting (ASTM E1049-85)                                [BUILD]
  No established Rust crate found. The algorithm is short; we implement
  it against the standard's worked examples and cross-check with a
  Python reference (e.g. the rainflow package) [VERIFY package].
Miner's-rule damage with S-N curves per material                 [BUILD]
  S-N data for AGED in-service uPVC, AC and cast iron is poorly known
  (11_). Start with relative rankings.
Survival / hazard models for pipe failure (Cox, Weibull,
gradient-boosted survival)                                      [ASSESS]
  Train in Python (lifelines / scikit-survival) once failure records
  exist; deploy coefficients or ONNX models into Rust (06_).
Hierarchical Bayesian models across networks (L6)               [ASSESS]
  Shared priors for wave speed and failure rates by material and era.

4. Reproducibility rules
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Every stochastic run records its seed(s), sampler settings, model
    version and data references.
  - Convergence diagnostics (R-hat, effective sample size) are computed
    and stored with every MCMC result; a result without them is not
    reported.
