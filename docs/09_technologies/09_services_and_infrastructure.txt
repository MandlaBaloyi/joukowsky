SERVICES AND INFRASTRUCTURE
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 - for review

Principle (06_engine_architecture): the physics and signal cores are pure
libraries; services are thin shells around them. Nothing in this file
should contain physics.

1. Service stack
------------------------------------------------------------------------
tokio (async runtime)                                            [ADOPT]
  The de-facto Rust async runtime; required by most crates in 08_.
axum (HTTP APIs)                                                 [TRIAL]
tonic (gRPC between internal services)                          [ASSESS]
serde (+ serde_json, rmp-serde / MessagePack)                    [ADOPT]
  All config, scenario files and messages. Schemas versioned.
tracing (+ tracing-subscriber)                                   [ADOPT]
  Structured logs and spans from day one, including inside the solver
  (at debug level), so any event's processing can be reconstructed.
OpenTelemetry (metrics, traces export)                          [TRIAL]
  Latency per pipeline stage against targets C1 (detect in seconds,
  locate in tens of seconds, recommend within a minute).

3. Hosting in South Africa (data in-country, low latency)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Regions available (checked 2026-09-28):
  AWS          af-south-1, Cape Town (since 2020, 3 availability zones)
  Azure        South Africa North, Johannesburg
               (South Africa West, Cape Town: status unclear - [VERIFY])
  Google Cloud africa-south1, Johannesburg (since 2024)
DECIDED (TD-007): AWS af-south-1. Open the account only when the engine
is ready for the cloud - the 2026 free plan runs 6 months or until the
credits are spent, from account creation. Until then everything runs
locally (Postgres 18 + local files through object_store's filesystem
backend). Portability is kept: object_store and plain Postgres let us
leave AWS if we must.
Load-shedding note: cloud regions have their own generation; our risk is
the SENSOR side going dark, handled in software (06_ outages file).

4. Law and security
------------------------------------------------------------------------
POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)                  [ADOPT
                                                             as practice]
  Pressure data is mostly not personal information, but site addresses,
  operator names, contact details for alerts and smart-meter data can
  be. Keep data in SA regions, minimise personal fields, document
  lawful basis. Get legal advice before the first partner [VERIFY].
Critical-infrastructure security                                 [ADOPT
                                                             as practice]
  - Read-only by default: the engine does not actuate valves unless a
    partner explicitly enables it, with human confirmation (E6, P4).
  - TLS everywhere; per-source credentials; least privilege.
  - Secrets never in the repo; a secrets manager per environment.
  - Dependency audit (cargo-deny, cargo-audit) in CI (10_).
  - Signed releases and an SBOM per build [ASSESS tooling].

Sources checked 2026-09-28: AWS, Azure and Google Cloud SA region
