MACHINE LEARNING AND AI
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 - for review

Rule (from 05_learning_systems/learning_over_time.txt): a learned model is
adopted only if it beats the physics-only baseline on held-out events AND
its outputs remain physically plausible. ML refines physics; it never
replaces it (P1).

1. Split: train in Python, serve in Rust
------------------------------------------------------------------------
PyTorch (+ PyTorch Geometric for graph models)                   [TRIAL]
  ROLE  Training classifiers, surrogates, graph models. Where the
        research community is; fastest route from paper to experiment.
ONNX as the model exchange format                                [TRIAL]
ort (ONNX Runtime bindings for Rust)                             [TRIAL]
  ROLE  Run trained models inside the Rust engine.
  STATE 2.0.0-rc series in 2026; described by maintainers as production-
        ready but not API-stable [VERIFY at adoption]. Wraps Microsoft's
        ONNX Runtime (C++), so it adds a native dependency.
Burn (Rust deep learning framework, v0.20, Jan 2026)            [ASSESS]
  ROLE  Pure-Rust training AND inference; CPU SIMD and GPU backends via
        CubeCL. Attractive long-term (one language, deterministic
        builds, no native ONNX Runtime). Assess once models exist.
Gradient-boosted trees for the Stage B classifier               [TRIAL]
  Train in Python (LightGBM / XGBoost); export to ONNX or evaluate the
  tree ensemble in a small Rust evaluator of our own (trees are simple
  and fully auditable).

2. Model families on our research track
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)                        [ASSESS]
  Well studied for single-pipe water hammer (2022-2026), including
  unsteady-friction variants and transient reconstruction from sparse
  sensors. Network-scale PINNs remain small (~19 pipes reported).
Graph neural networks for water networks                        [ASSESS]
  - Physics-informed GNN for STEADY hydraulics (Ashraf et al. 2024):
    <1% error vs EPANET on L-Town, millisecond inference.
  - Spatiotemporal GCN surrogate for WATER HAMMER under pipe ruptures
    (Eng. Appl. Comput. Fluid Mech., Feb 2026): matches pressure
    histories including peak timing at far lower cost than MOC.
  => Update to 08_research R-5: transient graph surrogates are no longer
     completely unexplored, but no published model yet gives calibrated
     UNCERTAINTY, handles UNKNOWN wave speeds, or supports inversion
     (localisation/ITA). That remains our gap (11_).
Neural operators (FNO, DeepONet) as MOC surrogates              [ASSESS]
Sequence models (1-D CNN / transformer) on raw multi-sensor
windows for classification (Stage B/C)                          [ASSESS]
Grey-box: MOC + learned correction trained through our adjoint  [BUILD]
  Only possible because we own the solver and its gradients.

3. Sim-to-real (the known failure mode)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
08_research Q-A2: direct sim-to-real accuracy was 30%, 62.5% after
transfer learning. Our tooling therefore includes:
  - domain randomisation in the scenario generator (sim crate),
  - real noise and real sensor artefacts injected into simulations,
  - per-network fine-tuning with versioned models and shadow mode,
  - physics-based localisation as the primary method (needs no training).

4. MLOps (auditable learning, P5)                               [ASSESS]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Model registry with immutable versions, training-data references,
  code version and evaluation results. Candidates: MLflow, or a minimal
  registry of our own on object storage + Postgres. Decide when the
  first model is trained.

5. Large language models                                          [HOLD]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Possible later uses: plain-language event explanations for operators,
summarising repair reports into labels, assisting analysts. Never in the
detection, localisation or safety path - an LLM output is not physics
and cannot be verified the way P1/P4 require.
