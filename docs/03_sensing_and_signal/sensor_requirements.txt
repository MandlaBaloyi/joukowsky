SENSOR NODE REQUIREMENTS (FIRST DRAFT)
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 - for review

All figures are initial engineering targets derived from the physics in
02_ and 03_; to be validated on a lab rig before hardware is selected.

Pressure transducer
-------------------
  Range:        0-16 bar gauge (distribution) or 0-25 bar (high-pressure
                zones / pump discharge). Must survive surges: overpressure
                rating >= 2x range; burst rating higher.
                Must read BELOW 0 gauge (to -1 bar) to see down-surges and
                cavitation - i.e. a compound/sealed or absolute sensor,
                not a gauge sensor that clips at 0.
  Accuracy:     <= 0.25% FS total error band (0.1% preferred).
  Resolution:   <= 0.01 bar effective (after averaging/filtering).
  Bandwidth:    >= 1 kHz mechanical/electrical response (piezoresistive
                silicon types meet this; many cheap "industrial" sensors
                have heavy internal filtering - must check).
  Output:       digital (preferred) or 4-20 mA / ratiometric into a
                >= 16-bit ADC with anti-alias filter.
  Stability:    long-term drift <= 0.1% FS/yr; the engine will also
                estimate drift from data (05_learning_systems).
  Mounting:     tapping as close to the main as possible; short, rigid,
                air-free connection (a long flexible hose or trapped air
                acts as a low-pass filter and distorts the waveform).

Clock
-----
  GNSS receiver with PPS output disciplining the ADC sample clock;
  target timestamp error <= 100 microseconds (see time_synchronisation).
  Holdover oscillator (TCXO) for GNSS outages.

Compute
-------
  MCU or low-power SoC capable of: continuous filtering and detection at
  the surveillance rate; ring buffer of >= 2 minutes at capture rate;
  local storage for >= 7 days of events and summaries (connectivity
  outages).

Communications
--------------
  LTE-M or NB-IoT primary (national coverage [VERIFY for target sites]);
  LoRaWAN option for dense private sites with a local gateway.

Power
-----
  Mains where available, with battery backup sized for multi-hour
  load-shedding stages; otherwise battery + solar. Power budget is a
  first-order design constraint because of continuous kHz sampling.
  Note: the moments when power fails (pump trips) are exactly the moments
  we most need to be recording.

Optional channels
-----------------
  - Flow (existing meter pulses, or electromagnetic meter) - for
    burst quantification cross-check.
  - Hydrophone / accelerometer - acoustic leak noise (complementary
    method for slow leaks; research track).
  - Temperature - for wave speed and sensor compensation.

Enclosure
---------
  Underground chambers flood; IP68. GNSS antenna placement from a
  chamber is a real constraint (open question Q-S2).
