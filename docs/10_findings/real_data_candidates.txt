REAL DATA CANDIDATES
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 (2026-09-29) - for discussion with the owner; NOTHING
here has been downloaded, searched for or checked online yet

Rule: no download, extraction or install without the owner's explicit
approval (CLAUDE.md, TD-020). Approved files go only into data/incoming,
are recorded in data/manifest.csv with their SHA-256, never executed.
Every fact below is from memory and marked [VERIFY] until checked at the
source, with approval.

Checklist before asking for approval
------------------------------------
  1. Publisher and official source (not a mirror).
  2. Licence: may we use it commercially? must we credit it?
  3. Size on disk, and the format (plain text / CSV / .inp preferred; no
     installers, scripts or macros).
  4. What it PROVES: which gap (known_gaps.txt) or ladder rung it closes.
  5. Ground truth: does it say what really happened (where, when, how
     big)? Without truth it can test robustness, not accuracy.
  6. Personal data: addresses, customer names? (not expected; if present,
     do not keep).

Does it have to be South African data?
--------------------------------------
Not for the physics: a wave in a pipe obeys the same laws in Pretoria
and in Adelaide. Yes, eventually, for the CONDITIONS: load-shedding,
intermittent supply, local pipe materials and ages. So: foreign lab and
benchmark data first to prove the method; South African partner data to
prove it where we sell.

Candidates, in suggested order
------------------------------
C1  EPANET example networks (Net1, Net2, Net3)                  [VERIFY]
    Source   US EPA, shipped with EPANET; believed public domain.
    Size     A few KB each, plain text.
    Proves   Our .inp reader on files written by real tools, not by us.
             Layouts only - no pressure recordings.
    Risk     Lowest.

C2  L-Town (BattLeDIM 2020 competition)                          [VERIFY]
    Source   Published by the competition organisers (University of
             Cyprus KIOS centre and partners), on Zenodo; believed CC BY.
    Content  An EPANET model of a realistic town (tens of km of pipe) and
             pressure/flow readings every 5 minutes for about two years,
             with the true leak times and places.
    Size     Believed hundreds of MB [VERIFY before approval].
    Proves   Reader and engine at town scale (G-10); Tier 0 behaviour;
             slow-leak methods (G-12). NOT transients - 5 min is far too
             slow to see a wave (P2).

C3  Published laboratory water-hammer experiments                [VERIFY]
    Source   University pipe rigs (e.g. Adelaide: Simpson, Bergant,
             Lambert) - numbers and figures in journal papers; some with
             supplementary data files.
    Proves   Our simulator against REAL physics with known truth (G-01,
             G-02 plastic pipes if a PE rig, G-03 unsteady friction).
    Note     Many numbers can be typed in from the papers - no download
             of data files needed, only reading.

C4  TSNet (Python transient simulator)                           [VERIFY]
    A tool, not data: needs a Python install and a package download.
    Proves   Independent cross-check of our simulator (G-01).

C5  Open high-frequency field transient datasets                 [VERIFY]
    Unknown whether any exist openly (08_ R-3, V-B3). Finding out needs
    a web SEARCH, which itself asks permission. If none exist, the data
    we collect with partners becomes a contribution.

C6  A South African partner site (the real prize)
    A campus, mall or estate that already has, or will install,
    transient loggers (~128 S/s) and shares the CSV exports. Ground truth
    by agreement: open a hydrant at a known place and time. This is rung
    5 - live shadow mode.

C7  Public weather data (for external_factors.txt E3/E4)         [VERIFY]
    Rainfall and temperature, for the pipe-health model later.
