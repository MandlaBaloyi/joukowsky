Checklist before asking for approval
------------------------------------
  1. Publisher and official source (not a mirror).
  2. Licence: may we use it commercially? must we credit it?
  3. Size on disk, and the format (plain text / CSV / .inp preferred; no
     installers, scripts or macros).
  4. What it PROVES: which gap (known_gaps.txt) or ladder rung it closes.
  5. Ground truth: does it say what really happened (where, when, how
     big)? Without truth it can test robustness, not accuracy.
  6. Personal data: addresses, customer names? (not expected; if present,
     do not keep).

Does it have to be South African data?
--------------------------------------
Not for the physics: a wave in a pipe obeys the same laws in Pretoria
and in Adelaide. Yes, eventually, for the CONDITIONS: load-shedding,
intermittent supply, local pipe materials and ages. So: foreign lab and
benchmark data first to prove the method; South African partner data to
prove it where we sell.
