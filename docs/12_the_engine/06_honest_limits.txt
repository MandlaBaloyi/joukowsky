HONEST LIMITS - WHAT IS PROVEN AND WHAT IS NOT
STATUS: DRAFT v0.1 (2026-10-01) - read before any claim to a client

Proven (on 2026-10-01)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The physics, against exact answers: Joukowsky's jump, wave periods,
    friction, plastic walls, pumps, valves, tanks, canals, refilling,
    vapour and gas cavities (V-A1 .. V-A19) - most to better than one
    part in a million.
  The wave solver against an independent method (transfer matrices):
    within 0.15% on a branched network (V-X1).
  The whole engine on simulated sites (the V-S cases built so far - V-S3,
    a trained classifier, is still a plan): bursts of 3-12 mm
    on an estate - detected 100%, called bursts 95-100%, located within
    about 0.1-0.4 m (wave speeds known) or 5-7 m (3% off, declared), the
    90% region holding the truth 88-95% of the time, sized within 1-7%;
    no false alerts in 9 logger-hours of quiet with slow wander; pump
    trips, valve closures, refills, supply cuts, column separation,
    clock errors, late uploads, gaps, power cuts at the gateway.
  Files: EPANET networks and logger exports as written by hand-made
    realistic files; a whole site through `jk replay` (V-S11).

Not proven - what real data must still show
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Everything above is on SIMULATED data, from our own simulator (now
    cross-checked by an independent method, but not yet by another
    team's code or by laboratory measurements - gap G-01).
  Real noise is not Gaussian, real pressure moves for many reasons
    (users, pumps starting, valves operated, air): the false-alert rate
    on a real network is unknown (G-05, G-11).
  Real wave speeds are unknown until measured; with 5% doubt the 90%
    regions are tens of metres long on long mains (FN-044).
  Real logger files will differ from ours (formats, time zones, gaps,
    clocks) - every reader assumption is reported, but some files will
    need new handling. 2026-10-05: the first real files (EPANET's own
    examples and L-Town, data/README.md) found three gaps in the network
    reader and two in the detector and logger reader on the first day
    (FN-045, FN-046) - all fixed; more will come.
  Events not yet simulated cannot be classified: pump STARTS and valve
    OPENINGS (a valve opening looks like a burst), sensor faults.
  Big real networks: the solvers run 1 000 km in seconds, but placement
    and location on a real municipal network are untested.

Assumptions to verify (marked [VERIFY] in the code and docs)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  wave-speed doubt 5%; negative pressure counted from -1 m; the empty-pipe
  threshold 2 m for 60 s; the refill window 15 min; vapour pressure at
  20 C; S-N exponent 4 for fatigue; PN ratings vs allowable surge per
  material (SANS 966 / SANS 4427); free-gas content of SA supply water;
  the collapse-peak factor of about 2 (FN-035) against lab data.

Not built yet (10_findings/known_gaps.txt)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Safe action advice (what to close, how slowly - P4); feedback from
  operators; the learning loops (per network, over time); a database
  and live connections (MQTT, SCADA); fatigue per pipe between loggers;
  locating events whose fronts are shaped by boiling; a model for long
  boiling zones; cavitation on by default; unsteady friction.
